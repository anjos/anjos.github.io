Talk at the Valais/Wallis AI Workshop: reproducibility in research

A talk at the Valais/Wallis AI workshop on reproducible research.

24 March 2017

This talk leverages experience from the development of Bob, a framework for reproducible research, and the BEAT platform, a web platform for the same purpose. It describes how the two are connected, and the model they implement for reproducible research in data sciences.
