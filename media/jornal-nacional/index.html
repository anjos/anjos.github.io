Interview for Jornal Nacional

An interview for a report on Jornal Nacional, Brazil's nationwide 20h00 news programme, on the start-up of CERN's LHC.

10 September 2008

This short report was aired on 10 September 2008 as part of the celebrations around the start-up of CERN’s Large Hadron Collider. Jornal Nacional is Brazil’s nationwide 20h00 news programme. I appear towards the end of the piece, answering one of the interviewer’s questions.
