Forum des 100: démocratiser l'accès à la puissance de l'IA

An intervention at the 20th Forum des 100, the annual gathering organised by Le Temps, on democratising access to the computing power behind AI.

31 October 2024

An intervention at the twentieth edition of the Forum des 100, the annual gathering organised by Le Temps, held in Lausanne on 31 October 2024 under the theme À quoi sert la Suisse romande?. The forum brings together around a thousand figures from Swiss economic, political, scientific and cultural life.

The talk, Démocratiser l’accès à la puissance de l’IA, presented a project supported by the Loterie Romande on widening access to the computing infrastructure that modern artificial intelligence depends on.
