Teaching

Graduate-level courses and summer schools I teach, most recent first.

  • Master in Artificial Intelligence: Modules M05, M06 and M08

    Idiap & Unidistance · 2019–2023

    Three modules taught between 2019 and 2023 to master students of the Master in AI run by the Idiap Research Institute with Unidistance: open science and ethics (M05), and machine learning fundamentals (M06 and M08).

  • Fundamentals of Statistical Pattern Recognition

    EPFL, Lausanne · 2013–2023

    EE-612 at EPFL: fundamental tools of machine learning and pattern recognition, from basics to deep learning, for Ph.D. students. Given biennially from 2013 to 2023.

  • IFI/UZH Summer School: From Scripts to Reusable Software and Reproducible Research

    University of Zurich · 2022

    Summer school at the University of Zurich: actionable guidelines for turning non-reproducible scripts with hard-coded parameters into reusable software, with examples in Python.

  • Reproducible Research for Pattern Recognition

    UNESP Bauru, Brazil · 2015

    A hands-on course given at UNESP Bauru, Brazil, on reproducible research for engineers working with software in pattern recognition and machine learning.
